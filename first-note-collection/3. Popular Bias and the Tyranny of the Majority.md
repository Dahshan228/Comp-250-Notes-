# Title
Popular Bias and the Tyranny of the Majority.

# Quotation

"Yet the sheer volume of content used to train the model creates an intellectual problem. Deep Research tends to draw on ideas that are frequently discussed or published, rather than the best stuff. Information volume tyrannises information quality."

# Citation

"The Danger of Relying on OpenAI's Deep Research." *The Economist*, February 13, 2025. https://www.economist.com/finance-and-economics/2025/02/13/the-danger-of-relying-on-openais-deep-research.

# Commentary

The model has a built in bias that treats popular opinions as facts, it repets common beliefs instead of good expert research. 
By relying on massive amounts of easily accessible internet data, the tool risks ignoring detailed debates and spreading oversimplified misconceptions.
