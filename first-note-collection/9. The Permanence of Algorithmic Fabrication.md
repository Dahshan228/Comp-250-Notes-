# Title
The Permanence of Algorithmic Fabrication

# Quotation

"Despite our best efforts, they will always hallucinate," ... "That will never go away."

# Citation

Metz, Cade, and Karen Weise. "A.I. Is Getting More Powerful, but Its Hallucinations Are Getting Worse." *The New York Times*, May 5, 2025. https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/05/technology/ai-hallucinations-chatgpt-google.html.

# Point Locator

Statement by Amr Awadallah, chief executive of Vectara and former Google executive. Online edition, unpaginated.

# Commentary

Researchers in the industry agree that AI hallucinations are permanent features, not temporary flaws. 
Because generative models predict words based on statistics instead of obtaining facts from a database, hallucination is the model's default behavior when there are no data limits.

# Keywords

algorithmic fabrication, generative architecture, probabilistic generators, human oversight
