# Title
High Volume Brainstorming and Momentum

# Quotation

"Work that will keep you moving forward. Little things often block our way, and a push might be all we need to accomplish it. When writing prior to AI, I might get stuck on a sentence and walk away from writing for an hour, but now I ask AI give me thirty distinct ways to end this sentence."

# Citation

Mollick, Ethan. "15 Times to Use AI, and 5 Not To." *One Useful Thing* (blog), December 9, 2024. https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/15-times-to-use-ai-and-5-not-to.

# Point Locator

# Commentary

AI is very effective for quick and easy tasks like generating alternate idea when you are creatively stuck. It is useds as inspiration to overcome the initial roadblock by instantly offering alot of phrasing or structural options, allowing the user to maintain momentum while still having control over the final selection.
