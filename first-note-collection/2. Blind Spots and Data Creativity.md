# Title
Blind Spots and Data Creativity.

# Quotation

"When it comes to data questions requiring more creativity, however, the model struggles. It wrongly estimates the average amount of money that an American household headed by a 25- to 34-year-old spent on whisky in 2021, even though anyone familiar with the Bureau of Labour Statistics data can find the exact answer ($20) in a few seconds."

# Commentary

Deep Research struggles with data questions that involve creativity or industry expertise, compared to asking it direct questions. It often fails to discover and analyze figures from complex statistical databases, making it ineffective for complicated observational studies that needs context and understanding.
