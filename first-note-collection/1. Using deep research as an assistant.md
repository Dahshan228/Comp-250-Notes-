# Title
Using deep research as an assistant.

"The obvious conclusions first. Deep Research is unable to conduct primary research, from organising polls in Peru to getting a feel for the body language of a chief executive whose company you might short."

# Commentary

One of the main limitations of using Deep Research as an assistant is that it is only a secondary research tool. 
It works best for the complex review of already published information rather than carrying out original primary research 
because it is unable to generate new, real world data.
