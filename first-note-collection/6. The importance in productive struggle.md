# Title
The Importance of Productive Struggle

# Quotation

"Asking AI to solve a problem for you is not an effective way to learn, even if it feels like it should be. To learn something new, you are going to have to do the reading and thinking yourself, though you may still find an AI helpful for parts of the learning process."

# Citation

Mollick, Ethan. "15 Times to Use AI, and 5 Not To." *One Useful Thing* (blog), December 9, 2024. https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/15-times-to-use-ai-and-5-not-to.

# Point Locator

"5 Times Not to Use AI," item 1: "When you need to learn and synthesize new ideas or information." Online edition, unpaginated.

# Commentary

When learning new concepts, the effort of reading, putting together, and processing material is important to a good understanding of the subject. Using AI to skip these processes prioritizes quick efficiency over comprehension, reducing the productive struggle needed to process and retain knowledge.

# Keywords

productive struggle, active learning, comprehension, knowledge retention
