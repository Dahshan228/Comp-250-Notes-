# Title
The Escalation of Inaccuracy in Reasoning Systems

# Quotation

"The newest and most powerful technologies — so-called reasoning systems from companies like OpenAI, Google and the Chinese start-up DeepSeek — are generating more errors, not fewer. As their math skills have notably improved, their handle on facts has gotten shakier."

# Citation

Metz, Cade, and Karen Weise. "A.I. Is Getting More Powerful, but Its Hallucinations Are Getting Worse." *The New York Times*, May 5, 2025. https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/05/technology/ai-hallucinations-chatgpt-google.html.

# Point Locator

Opening section (lede), immediately following the Cursor tech-support anecdote. Online edition, unpaginated.

# Commentary

When AI models advance in complicated logic and math, their fact reliability lowers. 
Because engineers optimize these reasoning systems for step by step problem solving, their understanding on objective truth fades, which leads to outputs that are logically sound but factually wrong.
