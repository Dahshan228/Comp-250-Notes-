# Title
The Compounding Error of Step-by-Step Logic
# Summary

The chain of thought method increases the chances for failure. In a multi step process, a single wrong premise early on becomes the foundation for everything that follows, creating a complicated system that has a foundation built on wrong info.
# Evidence

"Some hypothesize that it has to do with the reasoning models spending time “thinking” step by step through complex problems, before making up their answer. This seems to increase the rate of hallucinations at each step."

# Citation

The New York Times A.I. Hallucinations Are Getting Worse, Even as New Systems Become More Powerful
