# Title
Losing Critical Thinking by Outsourcing Research.
# Summary

Outsourcing research to an AI could weaken your critical thinking, because actively working with data is how real discoveries happen. Using automated tools to summarize everything removes the struggle to read everything and could possibly skip things that researchers spot like contradictions in data and come up with original ideas.

# Evidence

"For many jobs, researching is thinking: noticing contradictions and gaps in the conventional wisdom. The risk of outsourcing all your research to a supergenius assistant is that you reduce the number of opportunities to have your best ideas."
